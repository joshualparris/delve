How to Play

Core flow

Pick a route across 7 stages. Rooms include combat, treasure, events, short rests, and a final boss.

Turn structure

Each hero gets movement, one action, one bonus action when relevant, and one reaction each round. Opportunity attacks fire automatically when someone leaves melee reach. Some defensive reactions also auto-trigger.

Touch controls

Tap a hero to inspect them. On that hero’s turn, tap Move then a highlighted tile to move. Tap an ability button, then tap a valid target or tile. No hover is required.

Undo

Undo rewinds the current hero turn before random results are revealed. Move first, then undo if you want to reposition. Once dice resolve, that turn is locked in.

Conditions

Prone, stunned, poisoned, restrained, invisible, frightened, and burning all matter. Watch the log and condition chips.

Resources

Fighter uses Grit, Rogue uses Cunning, Cleric uses Faith, Wizard uses Arcana. Short rests recover some of these resources, but only twice per run.

Board language

Pillars and walls block line of sight. Mire slows movement. Spikes hurt on entry. Necrotic sigils punish ending your turn there. Shrines heal once. Barrels explode.

Win condition

Defeat the Sigil Tyrant and keep enough of your party standing to finish strong.
